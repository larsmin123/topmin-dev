What we do
01

Collection photography

Pim photographs every object on its own and sets the light, background and focus for each piece. We start with a plan that fits what your museum wants.

02

Digitisation

We bring the collection online with its data and metadata in order, so you can search, zoom in and reuse images freely.

03

Websites

We also design and build the museum’s own website, from identity to the last page. We are doing this now for Japanmuseum SieboldHuis.

04

Print

Images that hold up in print too, for books, catalogues and exhibitions.

05

Social media

From strategy to delivery. We make your collection a source of wonder in your own channels, with videos, carousels and details. At the Rijksmuseum, Lars reached 70 million people a year this way.

Position

A collection belongs to everyone.

What a museum keeps should also be on view online: free, sharp and easy to find. We do that well, or we don’t do it.

About

The best, with less.

TOP/MIN is the studio of Pim Top and Lars Minnes. In Dutch, top means the best and min means less. That is how we work: we take away what stands between a collection and its public.

People
Photography

Pim Top

Pim has photographed objects, heritage and exhibitions for more than fifteen years. His images are sharp enough to do research on. His work has appeared in ELLE Decoration, Wallpaper* and PIN-UP.

Content and strategy

Lars Minnes

Lars is an art historian and has worked for years as a content and social media specialist, for the Rijksmuseum and Fenix among others. With his trained eye he finds connections between images that others miss. He can do the same with your collection.
